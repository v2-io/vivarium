# Active hotspots — evolving next-work ideation (not geography, not claim law)

A living scratch surface for **what looks highest-leverage to do next**, with enough rationale that a concurrent mind can argue with the ranking instead of reverse-engineering it from chat. Complements the open-problem census (what is *open*) and CONSOLIDATION-STATUS (big-picture intuition): this segment is **judgment in motion** — TODO ideation that is allowed to be wrong, dated, and replaced.

It is **not** claim canon, not a build plan Joseph has ratified, and not a second outline. "Hotspot" here means *where attention and work are concentrating or should concentrate* — our activity — deliberately **not** a place on the planet.
