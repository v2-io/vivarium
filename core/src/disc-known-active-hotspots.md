## Discussion

Open lists without ranking become infinite; rankings without rationale become tribal. This segment is the small place where someone can write "do this next, because …" and the next mind can update the date and the argument without pretending a formulation was sealed.

The 2026-07-30 rank thrash (mixed-tier → weak predicates → regula → status-exists-so-convict-one-promise) is itself evidence: **run the instrument before inventing the missing one.** Status already is the conformance report; what it does not do is *gate*. Connectivity already is the ocean law in the router; what it does not do is *share*.

The molten-code half remains useful when digests move this fast — but FE(6) is supporting, not the identity of the page. When the probe registry makes era drift mechanical, FE(6) should thin or retire; FE(5) stays as long as concurrent minds need a dated leverage argument.
