# Probes before claims

No physics or world-behavior claim without a probe that can fail. Plausibility dressed as verification is a process failure.

## Formal Expression

1. **Probe requirement.** A claim about what the world does (flow, erosion, conservation, seam behavior, climate, land fraction, …) is not assertable as established until a probe exists that would fail if the claim were false.
2. **A probe that cannot fail is not a probe.** The physics must actually execute at the probe's footprint; a ratio of zeros, a no-op on submarine cells, or a tolerance sized to hide the defect is not evidence.
3. **Confirming numbers are suspect.** A result that matches prior expectation deserves more scrutiny than a surprise, not less.
4. **Rationalization is not diagnosis.** Explanations that preserve a preferred narrative (e.g. "erosion is invisible at macro scale") without checking the enabling condition (e.g. uplift zero, footprint underwater) are rejected when found.

## Working Notes

- Worked counter-examples stay in DECISIONS / history layer; do not re-narrate them here unless needed as regression guards in Working Notes of a physics segment.
- **Where probe *subjects* come from is upstream of this norm and not owned anywhere.** This norm governs what happens once someone suspects something; it says nothing about how the suspicion arises, and a probe can only convict a question already asked. The complementary channel — a trained eye finding an anomaly nobody had thought to declare ( #norm-no-depiction-without-referent FE(2) ) — now has an affordance: `vivarium explore`'s sighting capture (`C`) writes everything true at that instant, beside the world and never in the store, against a blank "What looked wrong" heading. **Deliberately not written up as a segment yet:** the pipeline (eye fires → sighting → probe → claim) has one built affordance and *zero completed round-trips*, and a norm for a method nobody has yet used end to end would be exactly the kind of claim this project asks not to make. The first sighting that becomes a probe that convicts a claim is what would earn it a home — and would say more about the method than speculation can.
