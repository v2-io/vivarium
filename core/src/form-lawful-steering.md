# Lawful steering: exo-authored realization of a fated draw

An exo agent may choose the realization of a fated-noise draw — conserving, in-distribution, phenomenally traceless, and always recorded — as the third mutation mechanism beside law-authoring and endo action; the goal-directed form has a named mathematical home and is held at sketch strength.

## Formal Expression

1. **The mechanism.** *Lawful steering* ( #lexicon/term/lawful-steering — an exo agent choosing which realization a fated draw takes; the term names the action, not the actor) is the **third mutation mechanism** for a world's true $\Omega$-trajectory. The other two are law-authoring — which does not mutate a kingdom but forks a new one ( #lexicon/term/law-is-identity) — and ordinary endo action within the law. Steering is the only exo channel that changes the trajectory *without* forking identity.
2. **Properties (each is a constraint, not a description).** A steering event **obeys conservation**, stays **in-distribution** for the draw it overrides, and leaves **no phenomenal trace** — no in-world observation distinguishes a steered draw from a fated one. A "steering" that violates any of these is not lawful steering; it is an unLawful edit wearing the name.
3. **Permissibility.** Steering is on the **permissible side** of the moratorium: directed-lawful $\ne$ unLawful-bug. The harm the moratorium targets is the kingdom failing its own coherence — **incoherence, never authorship** ( #scope-moratorium-endogenous-emergence; ETHICS.md "Guardrail against overreach", #lexicon/term/lawful-steering). A steered draw is coherent by construction (FE 2).
4. **The recording obligation.** Every steering event is a **recorded forcing** — logged so the run stays replayable (*Open-with-recorded-forcing*). Steering enters the world's interventional history ( #lexicon/term/interventional-history) and is part of the intervention script an *in vivia* citation must carry ( #form-in-vivia-citation). An unrecorded steering event breaks replay and is therefore unlawful under FE(2)'s traceless clause read together with determinism ( #post-determinism-as-ontology): traceless *inside* the world, never traceless *of* the world.
5. **Goal-directed steering (sketch).** "Specify a result at time $T$; solve for the lawful micro-steering that realizes it" has a precise mathematical home: the Doob $h$-transform / Schrödinger bridge / reverse-time SDE family. Unlanded — no derivation or implementation exists in this repository.
6. **Intractability as protection (discussion).** The improbability of a goal-directed steering makes it astronomically costly to compute — the difficulty is itself a structural protection against casual trajectory-authoring. Corollary, same tier: a fully-lawful native avatar is *born* via a developmental pathway inside the world's law, not conjured by steering.

## Discussion

Why a third mechanism is load-bearing: determinism-as-ontology makes the world a pure function of (seed, keys), which threatens to leave an author only two levers — fork the law or act as a creature. Steering is the narrow lawful middle: authorship over *which* possible world, exercised entirely inside the distribution the law already declares. That is also why the moratorium does not touch it — the guardrail says aporia is not the harm and authorship is not the harm; only incoherence is.
