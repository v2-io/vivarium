**Real unlanded meat (tier 1):**

Natural homes: the probe on `#detail-structure-scheme-map` (it conditions its WB and entropy rows) or a DECISIONS lead entry beside 852; the fork bears on `#form-grid-equiangular-staggered`. Read-primary source, PDF registered (`montoya-2026-covariant-sphere`).
2. **§4.8 + §9 "resolve before B1" — the ABF RT0 trap and the Bochev–Ridzal exoneration question.** Zero carriage (grepped Raviart/RT0/Bochev/non-affine across DECISIONS + core + LEXICON).
