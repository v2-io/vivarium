## Scope — how the question was decomposed

_The question spans two literatures that rarely cite each other — safety-critical display/alerting engineering (which has standards with clause numbers and decades of measured evidence) and game HUD/telemetry design (which is mostly craft consensus with a thin empirical layer) — plus two cross-cutting questions the asker flagged: how much a panel may display before it displays nothing, and how a display honestly announces its own unreliability. The angles therefore split the safety-critical side by discipline (alarm management, flight deck, ecological/control-room), give the game side two passes (HUD taxonomy + games-user-research evidence; telemetry/analytics/dashboard visualization), and reserve one angle for the display-integrity questions (uncertainty/staleness annunciation and measured clutter–density tradeoffs) that cut across both._
