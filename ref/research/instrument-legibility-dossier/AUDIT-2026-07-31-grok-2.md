# Audit — Instrument legibility dossier

**Auditor:** Grok (xAI), session of 2026-07-31  
**Artifact under audit:** `ref/research/instrument-legibility-dossier/` (ladder docs `00`–`04` + `appendix/`)  
**Mode:** de novo. Adjudicated against a complete, usable, and completely truthful idealization of the target (defined below).  
**Constraint:** only this file and the assigned scratch directory were written; the report under audit was not modified.

---

## Idealization of the target

What this dossier is *for*, idealized:

1. **Complete (within a named scope).** Map what is actually known — theoretically or empirically — about making a real-time instrument legible to a human operator who is already doing a hard visual primary task. Scope is the avionics/SCADA layer wearing game clothing: not game feel, onboarding, or motivation. Completeness includes honest absence: domains searched and found empty, domains not searched, and the specific unmeasured case the consumer cares about.
2. **Usable.** A later reader (or implementer) can (a) find the load-bearing result in under a minute, (b) recover the primary without re-deriving the search, (c) know what may be designed against today versus what is still a lead, and (d) re-run perishable inventory without re-reasoning.
3. **Completely truthful.** Grades match evidence. Quotes survive primary contact. Glosses do not wear the authority of measurements. Design consequences are labeled as inferences. Refutations are retained so they are not re-derived. Distance from source is stated where it binds. Numbers that can be invented are not invented.

The dossier self-declares as **reference-grade research substrate**, not vivarium claim canon. That declaration is part of the ideal: truthfulness includes not minting architecture law from a research brief.

**Overall verdict (one line):** This is an unusually honest, usable research product for an LLM-mediated harvest stopped mid-verification; its truth-discipline is its strongest asset; its completeness is partial by method and by domain; its usability is high in the Bevy half and medium in the human half because ~87% of extracted claims remain unvoted and many load-bearing human results still sit one remove from a read primary.

## Part 1 — Index (`00-INDEX.md`)

### What the ideal index does

States the question, the ladder (half-life ordering), the grading scheme, the verification debt ledger, the production method and its failure modes, and the non-claim scope — without substituting for the ladder or the appendix.

### Strengths (truth)

- **Half-life ladder is the right operational axis.** Ordering by what decays (decades / decade / major release / months) is better than abstract→concrete for maintenance, and the index enforces it.
- **Epistemic grading is explicit and usable:** `[T]/[E]/[C]/[REV]` plus verification tags (`VERIFIED` / `CONTESTED` / `REFUTED` / `UNVOTED` / later `SOURCE-READ`). The quote-over-gloss-over-consequence rule is not decoration; it is the measured failure shape of this production method, and naming it is itself a finding.
- **Verification debt is centralized.** One ledger for all four ladder documents, ranked, with “none known-wrong” stated carefully. Fragmenting debts would have laundered cleanliness into each part.
- **Production honesty is load-bearing.** Stopped mid-verification (~244 agents), 49 adjudicated of ~388 extracted, 17/49 refuted, two outright fabrications recorded with source-shape analysis, Healey and Wolfe GS6 burned or discounted with reasons. The index does not pretend the surviving 25 are the whole corpus.
- **§4a (“distance from source”) is rare and correct.** Admitting that the indexing session read no primary, that each hop can shed a qualifier, and that the appendix is closer to the evidence than the distillations, is exactly the discipline an ideal index needs. The overlay-expectancy p-sign misprint episode (caught and restored) is evidence the discipline was applied at least once under pressure.
- **Scope fence is clear.** Research only; no vivarium claims; no promotion decision. That matches vivarium’s segment-canon rule and prevents this folder from becoming a second law.

### Defects and soft spots

| ID | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| I-1 | Medium | **“~388 / ~339 unvoted” is approximate and not re-derivable from the shipped tree.** The appendix syntheses say ~111 and ~123 unvoted for two runs; Bevy’s unvoted remainder is not tallied the same way. An ideal index would ship a claim ledger (CSV/JSON) so counts are greppable rather than remembered. |

Against the ideal index: **strong pass on truth posture**, **pass on usability of the map**, **partial on completeness of provenance artifacts**. This is the best single file in the set for teaching a reader how not to be misled by the rest.

## Cross-cutting findings

### C1. Truth discipline is unusually high for the production method

The method (parallel search → extract → 3-vote adversarial verification → synthesis, stopped early) is known to produce confident falsehoods. This dossier’s distinctive achievement is **not** that it avoided errors — it recorded a 35% refutation rate on adjudicated claims and two fabrications — but that it **built the errors into the product as signals** (burned sources, retained REFUTED tables, quote-over-gloss rule, distance-from-source warning). Relative to the ideal of complete truthfulness, that is the right failure mode: *transparent incompleteness* rather than *plausible completeness*.

### C2. Verification coverage is the dominant truth gap
